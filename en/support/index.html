Frequently Asked Questions

Getting Started
What does MarkPix do exactly?

MarkPix is a professional camera app that stamps location (address + coordinates), date-time, and optionally weather data onto every photo you take. This makes your photos provide evidence-grade answers to "when and where was this taken?" — used for field reports, insurance damage assessments, construction progress logs, legal documentation.

How do I get started?
  • Download MarkPix from the App Store.
  • Grant camera and location permission on first launch.
  • Select your field of work (for template suggestions, skippable).
  • Tap the capture button — the stamp is applied automatically.
Can I use MarkPix without location permission?

Yes, you can take photos without location permission, but the stamp won't include location/address — only date-time will be printed. Since MarkPix's main value is the location stamp, we recommend granting "While Using App" permission. The app never accesses location in the background.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Capturing photos, getting GPS coordinates, applying stamps — all work offline. Internet is only needed for address lookup (coordinate → "Brooklyn, NY") and weather data. Without internet, the stamp shows coordinates and date but the address field stays empty.

Stamp and Customization
How do I customize the stamp?
  • Settings → Stamp: default mode, opacity, position, color theme.
  • Settings → Stamp fields: which fields to print (address, altitude, coordinates, weather, etc.).
  • Tap the Templates chip on the camera screen to pick one of 11 ready-made industry templates.
What does "Save original" mean? Should I keep it on?

Yes, it's on by default. MarkPix saves the unstamped original alongside the stamped version. This lets you edit the photo later in another app if needed. Turn it off in Settings to save storage space.

The stamp location is wrong — can I correct it manually?

Yes. On the photo detail screen, tap "Adjust location" and drag the pin on the map. A new stamp is generated automatically. This is especially useful where GPS is weak (basements, underground parking).

Where does the weather data come from?

Live from Apple's WeatherKit service, queried at capture time using your location. Includes temperature, condition, humidity, pressure, wind direction and speed. Choose which fields appear on the stamp in Settings.

Subscription and Pricing
What's the difference between Free and Pro?
  • Free: 5 photos per day, Classic template, basic stamp, manual share.
  • Pro: Unlimited captures, 11 industry templates, PDF reports, drawing/annotation, stamped video, company logo, full customization.
How do I cancel my subscription?

iOS subscriptions are managed by Apple ID, not by MarkPix:

  • On your iPhone: Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions.
  • Find MarkPix → Cancel Subscription.
  • You keep Pro features until the end of the billing period.

Or go directly to: apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions

Can I get a refund?

MarkPix refunds are processed by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, select your MarkPix purchase, state the reason and submit. Apple typically decides within 48 hours. We cannot process refunds directly (Apple policy).

Can I use Lifetime on multiple devices?

Yes. The Lifetime purchase is tied to your Apple ID. All iPhone/iPad devices signed in with the same Apple ID will have Pro features. On a new device, open MarkPix and tap Settings → Restore Purchases to activate.

Why does it say "Restore Purchases" if I haven't bought Pro?

The Restore button is useful if you bought Pro on another device or your subscription was accidentally reset. If you've never bought Pro, you don't need to tap it — just use the Free tier.

Does it support Family Sharing?

Not yet — each subscription is tied to a single Apple ID. Family Sharing support is on the roadmap for late 2026.

PDF Reports and Sharing
How do I create a PDF report?
  • In the Gallery, tap "Select" and pick the photos for the report.
  • Tap "Create Report".
  • Add a title, description, optional company logo.
  • Choose a template (Standard / Insurance / Construction / Legal — Pro).
  • Save the PDF or share directly.
How many photos can I include in one PDF report?

No technical limit, but we've tested up to 50 photos reliably. More photos extend PDF generation time. For large reports (e.g., insurance claims), split photos into multiple PDFs.

How do I add my company logo?

Pro users: go to Settings → Stamp → Company logo, upload a PNG or JPG. The logo appears in the top-left of the stamp and on the PDF report cover. Transparent PNG is recommended.

Data and Privacy
Are my photos uploaded to your servers?

No. MarkPix has no servers. All your photos and location data are stored on your device in a SQLite database. Only coordinates are sent to Apple for address lookup, and to Apple WeatherKit for weather data — your actual photos never leave the device.

What anonymous statistics are collected?

Firebase Analytics collects anonymous usage data (which screen opened, which feature used). No identity info (Apple ID, name, email). You can disable this anytime in Settings.

Full details: Privacy Policy

How do I delete my data?

When you delete MarkPix, all photos, location data, and settings are removed from your device. We have no server, so no "data deletion request" is needed. To reset anonymous Firebase statistics, email support@getmarkpix.com with your device's anonymous ID.

Troubleshooting
GPS signal not working / stamp says "No location"
  • Step outside if you're indoors (GPS is weak inside).
  • Check iPhone Settings → Privacy → Location Services is on.
  • Verify Settings → MarkPix → Location is set to "While Using App".
  • Toggle Airplane Mode on and off to reset GPS.
  • Restart your iPhone — this resets the GPS chip.
Address is wrong — shows a nearby street

Apple Maps' reverse geocoding can be less accurate in rural areas. Use "Adjust location" on the photo detail screen to manually select the correct address. A new stamp is generated automatically.

The app crashes or freezes
  • Restart your iPhone.
  • Update MarkPix from the App Store (latest version?).
  • Still crashing? Email support@getmarkpix.com with iPhone model, iOS version, MarkPix version, and where the crash happens.
Photos don't appear in the gallery

MarkPix maintains its own gallery, separate from the iPhone Photos app. It only shows photos taken with MarkPix. If your MarkPix photos are also missing, you may have experienced data loss — email support@getmarkpix.com and we'll try to recover.

Battery drains quickly

Because the camera + GPS stay on, MarkPix can drain battery. For long field sessions, enable Low Power Mode, open the camera only when capturing, and send MarkPix to background between shots — this stops GPS.
