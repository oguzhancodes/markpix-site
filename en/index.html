FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are my photos uploaded to a server?
No. All photos, videos and location data are stored only on your device, in the app's own space. Location is queried against Apple Maps for address lookup; the result is processed on device too. See the privacy policy.
Is a brand logo or watermark added to my photo?
Never. No one — including free users — gets a "MarkPix" logo, brand name or forced watermark on their photo. No brand name in the file name either. The stamp is made only from the information you choose.
What's included in the free plan?
5 photos per day and the basic stamp modes — forever, ad-free. Upgrading to Pro removes the daily limit and unlocks the professional stamp, all templates, PDF reports and video stamping.
Is the stamped location and time admissible in court?
The MarkPix stamp is for personal record and information; GPS readings vary by device and conditions. For consequential legal/insurance use, seek independent verification (notary, expert). See Terms, section 12.
How do I cancel a subscription?
Through your Apple Account: Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
Which devices are supported?
Every iPhone and iPad running iOS 17.0 or later. An Android version is on the roadmap.
