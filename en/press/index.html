Company Boilerplate

Pick the length that fits your piece.

Short (1-2 sentences)

MarkPix is an ad-free, watermark-free iOS GPS camera app for field professionals. By stamping location, time, and weather data onto photos, it turns them into evidence-grade documents for construction, insurance, real estate, legal, and field service work.

Medium (paragraph)

MarkPix is a professional GPS camera app launched on iOS in 2026, positioned as the "ad-free, watermark-free, Turkish-first" alternative to market leader GPS Map Camera (which has 100M+ downloads). Built for construction contractors, insurance damage assessors, real estate agents, fiber/field technicians, and attorneys — anyone whose photos need to answer "when and where was this taken?" with confidence. It includes 11 industry-specific templates, PDF report generation, drawing and annotation, and stamped video recording. All photos and location data stay on the user's device — there are no company servers, ensuring full GDPR and KVKK compliance.

Long (press release body)

MarkPix is a GPS-stamped camera app for iOS, released in May 2026, focused on the "photo as evidence" needs of field professionals. Existing solutions in this App Store category — most notably GPS Map Camera with 100M+ downloads — fell short for professional use due to aggressive ad strategies, forced watermarks, and lack of Turkish-language support. MarkPix directly addresses these gaps.

The app automatically stamps each photo with location (address + GPS coordinates + Plus Code), date-time, and optional weather data (temperature, condition, humidity, pressure, wind). The stamp is fully customizable: opacity, position, color theme, field visibility — all under user control. Eleven industry-specific templates (Construction Record, Damage Assessment, Real Estate Presentation, Field Service, Legal Evidence, Time Tracking, Logistics, Agriculture, Event, Travel, Classic) serve the needs of distinct professional groups.

What sets MarkPix apart technically: all data stays on-device. The company has no servers; photos are never uploaded to any cloud. This architecture ensures GDPR/KVKK compliance and protects the security of sensitive field imagery (private property, interior damage shots, legal evidence photos).

Developer Oğuzhan TANRIVERDİ, a software engineer and ISP operator based in Manisa, Türkiye, designed the product from firsthand field experience: "I was using GPS Map Camera every day in the field, and every time the bad design, aggressive ads, and absence of Turkish frustrated me. MarkPix is my answer to that frustration."

MarkPix Free allows 5 photos per day. Three subscription options unlock unlimited use: Monthly $1.99, Yearly $11.99 (50% savings), and Lifetime $29.99. No ads, ever.
