1. Data Controller

For the purposes of GDPR, KVKK, CCPA and LGPD, the data controller is:

  • Name: Oğuzhan TANRIVERDİ
  • App: MarkPix (iOS)
  • Web: getmarkpix.com
  • Email: destek@getmarkpix.com
  • Jurisdiction: Istanbul, Türkiye

We do not have a designated Data Protection Officer (DPO) as our processing activities do not meet the GDPR Article 37 mandatory threshold. For all privacy enquiries, please contact us via email above.

2. What We Collect

2.1 Data processed only on your device

The following data is never sent to our servers. It is stored locally in the app's sandbox on your device:

  • Photos and videos: The images you capture and their stamps.
  • Location data: Latitude, longitude, altitude, accuracy (only while the camera is open and at the moment of capture).
  • Address information: Short and full address strings corresponding to your location.
  • Weather data: Temperature, condition, humidity, wind — embedded in the photo metadata.
  • Projects and tags: Project names, descriptions, tags and notes you create.
  • Attendance records: Clock-in/out times and locations.
  • App preferences: Language, theme, stamp settings, coordinate format, etc.

2.2 Data processed via Apple

The following is processed directly by Apple Inc.; the app is not a party to these processes:

  • Purchase records: Via Apple StoreKit — your Apple Account, purchase history, subscription status. Apple's privacy policy applies.
  • App Store analytics: Anonymous installation, crash and usage statistics collected by Apple App Analytics.

2.3 Data we do not collect

  • Name, surname, email, phone (the app does not require account creation).
  • Contacts, calendar, microphone (microphone is used only during video recording; audio is embedded in the file and not transmitted).
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA) or third-party ad tracking data — the app contains no advertising.
  • Cross-app or cross-site tracking.

3. How We Use Your Data

The data described above is used for the following purposes:

  • Adding GPS stamps, date, address and weather information to your photos (the app's core function).
  • Displaying your photos in the gallery and filtering by project.
  • Generating PDF reports and exports.
  • Verifying Pro/Lifetime subscriptions (via Apple).
  • Complying with legal obligations.

4. Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, UK or Switzerland, we process your personal data based on the following lawful bases under GDPR Article 6:

  • Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)): Processing required to deliver the service you requested (e.g. attaching GPS data to your photo).
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): Anonymous crash reports and Apple App Analytics, for improving app stability and quality.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): Camera, location and photo library permissions — you may grant or revoke these at any time via iOS Settings.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)): Compliance with tax, consumer-protection and other applicable laws.

5. Third-Party Services

5.1 Apple Inc. — MapKit / Reverse Geocoding

Coordinates are sent to Apple Maps services for address resolution. The query contains only coordinates; no personal identifier is attached. Apple's privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy

5.2 Apple Inc. — StoreKit (Purchases)

Pro subscriptions and Lifetime purchases are made through the Apple App Store. We neither see nor store your payment information. The purchase verification record returned by Apple is processed on your device to update subscription status.

5.3 Apple Inc. — Push Notifications

The app currently sends no push notifications. If this changes, this policy will be updated.

5.4 Weather Service

When the weather stamp feature is enabled, your coordinates are sent to a weather service provider as an anonymous query. No personal identifier is attached.

6. Data Retention

  • On-device data: Stored until you delete the app or remove the data from within the app.
  • Apple purchase records: Subject to Apple's retention policy.
  • Support emails: Emails sent to destek@getmarkpix.com are deleted within 2 years of issue resolution at the latest.

7. Data Security

  • All app data is encrypted using iOS Data Protection mechanisms (tied to device lock).
  • Because no photos or location data are stored on our servers, server breach risk does not exist for this data.
  • All communication with Apple uses HTTPS (TLS).

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Under GDPR (EU/EEA/UK/Switzerland):

  • Access — request a copy of your personal data (Art. 15)
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate data (Art. 16)
  • Erasure — "right to be forgotten" (Art. 17)
  • Restriction — limit how we use your data (Art. 18)
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format (Art. 20)
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21)
  • Withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7(3))
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

Under KVKK (Türkiye, Art. 11):

  • Learn whether your personal data is being processed
  • Request information about how your data is used
  • Learn the purpose and verify it is consistent
  • Request correction, deletion or destruction
  • Learn the third parties to whom data has been transferred
  • Object to processing carried out solely by automated means
  • Claim damages if you suffer loss due to unlawful processing

Under LGPD (Brazil):

  • Confirmation of processing existence
  • Access to your data
  • Correction of incomplete or outdated data
  • Anonymisation, blocking or deletion
  • Data portability
  • Information about the public and private entities with which we have shared your data
  • Revocation of consent

To exercise these rights: most of your data is on your device — use Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete all data in the app for instant local erasure. For other requests, email destek@getmarkpix.com. We will respond within the legal time limit (30 days for GDPR/LGPD, 30 days for KVKK, 45 days for CCPA).

9. Children's Privacy

MarkPix is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in some EU member states) and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If we learn that a child has used the app without verifiable parental consent, the relevant data will be deleted immediately. Please notify us via destek@getmarkpix.com.

10. International Data Transfers

On-device data is not sent outside your device. Queries to Apple services and purchase transactions may be processed on Apple's infrastructure in the European Union and/or the United States. Apple provides Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC) and other safeguards approved by the European Commission for such transfers.

11. California Residents (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under CCPA/CPRA:

  • Right to know what personal information we collect, use, disclose and sell.
  • Right to delete personal information we have collected from you.
  • Right to correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to opt-out of sale/sharing — we do not sell or share your personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising. There is therefore nothing to opt out of.
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising your rights.
  • Right to limit use of sensitive personal information — we do not use sensitive personal information beyond what is necessary to provide the requested service.

To exercise these rights, email destek@getmarkpix.com with the subject "CCPA Request".

12. Policy Changes

We may update this policy due to changes in applicable laws or new app features. Updates are published on this page with the updated "Last updated" date. Material changes will be notified in-app or by email where reasonably possible.

13. Contact

For any questions, requests or complaints regarding this policy or our processing of personal data:

  • Email: destek@getmarkpix.com
  • Postal jurisdiction: Istanbul Courts and Enforcement Offices, Türkiye
